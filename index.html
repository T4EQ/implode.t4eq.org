How to play Implode

→✨

Clear the board by removing groups of three or more connected blocks of the same color.

Blocks fall downward after each move, and empty columns slide left. Plan ahead so you do not leave isolated blocks.

  • Mouse or touch: select a block to remove its group.
  • Keyboard: use arrow keys to move, then Enter or Space.
  • Use Undo, Replay, or choose a new difficulty at any time.
